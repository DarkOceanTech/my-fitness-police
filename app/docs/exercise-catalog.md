# Exercise catalog — My Fitness Police

48 movements, catalog version 1, added September 10, 2026. The app's Exercises tab and workout picker use this same Room catalog.

**Interpretation.** Primary lists the intended major movers; secondary lists assisting movers and selected stabilizers. These categories are an anatomical interpretation for the described technique, not measured activation percentages, an exhaustive list of every active muscle, or a claim that one exercise is best. Joint position, grip, elbow path, support and load can change the relative contributions. Electromyography alone does not establish long-term muscle growth.

Equipment lists name items used together, unless explicitly separated by “or.” Bodyweight is included even when an apparatus (for example a pull-up bar) is needed. “Warmup,” “working,” and “superset” remain properties of workout sets, not of the exercise catalog.

The requested machine pull-back is named **Seated independent-arm machine row**, with both bilateral and unilateral use in its description. It assumes a seated horizontal row with two independent handles; it does not assume a particular brand, loading ratio or stack calibration. The other requested movements are **Bent-over barbell row** and **Seated close-grip cable row**.

## Anatomy and exercise references

Descriptions are newly written concise movement summaries. Muscle-role assignments combine standard joint anatomy with the cited exercise studies; roles not directly measured in a study are anatomical inferences, not research findings.

- [OpenStax — shoulder girdle and upper-limb muscle actions](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/11-5-muscles-of-the-pectoral-girdle-and-upper-limbs): anatomical names and actions for the upper limb.
- [OpenStax — pelvic girdle and lower-limb muscle actions](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/11-6-appendicular-muscles-of-the-pelvic-girdle-and-lower-limbs): quadriceps, hamstrings and ankle anatomy.
- [OpenStax — abdominal wall and thorax](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/11-4-axial-muscles-of-the-abdominal-wall-and-thorax): abdominal anatomy.
- [Lehman et al. — rowing and pulldown EMG study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC449729/): latissimus dorsi, elbow-flexor and scapular muscle involvement.
- [ACE-sponsored back exercise study](https://contentcdn.eacefitness.com/April2018/ACE_BackExerStudy.pdf): rows, pull-ups and back-muscle recruitment.
- [Hammer Strength — independent-arm row mechanism](https://www.lifefitness.com/en-us/catalog/strength-training/plate-loaded/iso-lateral-t-bar-row): independent arm motion as an equipment characteristic, not identification of the user's machine.
- [ACE-sponsored chest exercise study](https://contentcdn.eacefitness.com/certifiednews/images/article/pdfs/ACE_BestChestExercises.pdf): pectoralis major involvement in pressing and fly movements.
- [Comparison of push-up and bench-press kinematics and activation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6728153/): pressing muscle participation.
- [Overhead press EMG analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9354811/): deltoid and triceps involvement.
- [ACE-sponsored shoulder study](https://contentcdn.eacefitness.com/certifiednews/images/article/pdfs/ACEShoulderStudy.pdf): deltoid-region involvement in shoulder exercises.
- [ACE-sponsored biceps study](https://acewebcontent.azureedge.net/certifiednews/images/article/pdfs/ACE%20BicepsStudy.pdf): elbow-flexion exercises.
- [Squat, Romanian deadlift and hip thrust comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31356511/): knee and hip extensor involvement.
- [Leg press and Smith-machine squat study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30405437/): quadriceps involvement.
- [Conventional versus Romanian deadlift study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30662500/): different knee and hip demands.
- [Standing versus seated calf-raise training](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38156065/): knee position changes gastrocnemius length; both variants train the soleus. No claim that seated training produces greater soleus growth.

## Persistence

Room version 7 adds non-null `description`, `primaryMuscles` and `secondaryMuscles` text columns with empty defaults for legacy custom exercises. Equipment remains a text field. Multiple equipment or muscle entries use semicolons. The new fields flow through entity, DAO, repository, ViewModel, custom-exercise form, catalog and workout picker.

Creation and migration from version 6 insert stable catalog IDs. The six original seed IDs are enriched, preserving names, archive flags, changed equipment and workout references. User-created rows are preserved without inferring new descriptions from their names. New custom exercises require name, description, equipment and primary muscles; secondary muscles are optional. There is no destructive migration or automatic deletion of user data.
